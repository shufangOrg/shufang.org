## ⚡ TL;DR - 30 Second Overview

**What**: Native iOS app for 一个人的书房 (Chinese audiobook podcast platform)

**Why**: Bring high-quality literary audio content to mobile with native experience

**When**: 3-4 months to v1.0 launch

**How**: 
- Swift + UIKit (MVVM architecture)
- RSS feeds for content
- AVFoundation for audio
- Core Data for persistence

**Who**:
- Target: Chinese literature lovers (25-45 years)
- Team: 1-2 iOS devs, 1 designer, 1 tester
- Users: 1,000+ downloads in first month

**Features**:
- Browse episodes and books
- Full-featured audio player
- Download for offline
- Search and discovery
- Reader profiles
- Dark mode

**Cost**: 
- Development: $10k-$50k (or volunteer)
- Hosting: Minimal (RSS-based)
- Maintenance: <$1k/year
