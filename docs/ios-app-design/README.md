## 📱 About 一个人的书房

一个人的书房 is a Chinese audiobook podcast platform that has been operating since 2013. The platform features:
- Original book readings without background music
- Multiple podcast series (主播, 作家说, 声音邮局)
- Over 800 episodes with 45+ million downloads
- Focus on authentic voice and literary content

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

The iOS app design follows these core principles:
- **Simplicity**: Clean, minimalist interface that focuses on content
- **Accessibility**: Easy navigation for all age groups
- **Authenticity**: Preserve the "naked voice" philosophy of the platform
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Respect for Chinese language and literary traditions
